HR & Recruitment Coordination

Supporting recruitment, workforce planning and employee administration while keeping processes organized, responsive and aligned with business needs.

HR Administration Recruitment Workforce Planning Employee Coordination
THE CHALLENGE

Keeping people processes organized while supporting business growth

In HR support roles, I have worked across recruitment, employee coordination and administrative processes where delays or incomplete follow-up could affect staffing, employee experience and day-to-day operations.

The challenge was not simply to fill vacancies. It was to help ensure that recruitment activity, employee documentation, onboarding, workforce planning and follow-up were coordinated effectively.

30%

Reduction in time-to-hire following recruitment process improvements.

WHAT I DID

Coordinated the process from recruitment through employee support

01

Recruitment Coordination

Supported vacancy coordination, candidate communication, interview arrangements, recruitment documentation and follow-up with relevant stakeholders.

02

Workforce Planning

Supported workforce planning activities by identifying staffing gaps and helping align recruitment priorities with operational requirements.

03

Employee Administration

Assisted with employee records, leave management follow-up, onboarding, offboarding, staff data and documentation.

04

Employee Relations Support

Supported the administration and follow-up of disciplinary matters, grievances and employee concerns while maintaining appropriate confidentiality.

20% Reduction in hiring gaps through workforce planning support.
30% Reduction in time-to-hire after recruitment improvements.
18% Improvement in retention linked to employee engagement initiatives.
TOOLS & SKILLS

Capabilities demonstrated

Recruitment Coordination Interview Scheduling Employee Records Workforce Planning Onboarding Offboarding Leave Administration Staff Data Management Employee Engagement Confidential Documentation Stakeholder Follow-up HR Administration
OUTCOME

More structured people processes

The work contributed to more structured recruitment and employee administration, with measurable improvements in hiring gaps, time-to-hire and employee retention.

It also strengthened my ability to manage sensitive information, coordinate multiple stakeholders and follow tasks through to completion without requiring constant supervision.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

This case study demonstrates my ability to combine administrative accuracy with people coordination, structured follow-up and practical problem-solving. These skills are directly transferable to remote Executive Assistant, Virtual Assistant, HR Administrator and Operations roles.
